Platinum Kaizo Companion v3.3

Trainer cards tightened substantially on portrait. Six-Pokémon teams remain 3x2 in portrait and 6x1 in landscape/desktop. Trainer cards now have stronger borders, accent top edges, larger gaps and shadow separators between battles.

Trainer Pokémon sprites are now loaded eagerly, with PokeAPI sprite URLs first and Pokémon Showdown fallbacks, to avoid Safari lazy-loading failures inside the scrolling trainer pane.

Encounter timing is grouped inside the encounter method it belongs to. Morning/Day/Night grass, surf or fishing variants render within that method block rather than appearing as detached tables at the bottom. Story-order location navigation is unchanged.
