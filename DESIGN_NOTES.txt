Platinum Kaizo Companion v4.0

Refinement pass over v3.8.
- Core navigation/data behaviour preserved.
- Trainer cards keep all battles inline, 3-wide portrait / up to 6-wide landscape, now with trainer-class sprites and stronger battle separation.
- Encounter presentation keeps story order and nests Morning/Day/Night inside the relevant method with larger sprites.
- Move list now surfaces BP / accuracy / PP and a one-line effect summary before opening a move.
- Special > Changes retains category filters with stronger scan-friendly styling.
- Pokédex marks stat-modified species more clearly while retaining compact deltas.
- Cosmetic Sinnoh / Diamond / Pearl / Platinum polish across banners, panels, tabs and selected states.

Trainer-class sprites are loaded from Pokemon Showdown's public trainer sprite directory where a matching Gen IV/class sprite exists, with fallbacks for unsupported custom trainer names. Pokemon Showdown notes that many trainer sprites require artist credit; see its trainer-sprite credits directory for attribution.
