Platinum Kaizo Field Guide v2.0

Complete Sinnoh-first redesign, independent of the Origin HeartGold visual system.

Trainer Splits are parsed directly from:
Roark Split, Gardenia Split, Fantina Split, Maylene Split, Wake Split, Byron Split, Candice Split, Volkner Split, Galactic Split, Elite Four Split

Trainer battle sheets preserve the workbook's team structure:
- level / Pokémon
- nature / ability
- held item
- four moves
- AI flag chips where trainer-data flags can be matched

Stat rule:
89 (+10) means 89 is the final Platinum Kaizo stat and +10 is the change from vanilla.
