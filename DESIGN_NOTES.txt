Platinum Kaizo Companion v5.0

Field Manual refinement over v4.0.
- Core tab structure and source data preserved.
- Dynamic Sinnoh section identities and upgraded banners/panels.
- Global grouped search across Pokémon, encounters, trainers, moves and items (Ctrl/Cmd+K).
- Local favourites and recently viewed shortcuts.
- Two-Pokémon quick compare from the Pokédex.
- Pokédex now surfaces recorded Kaizo stat/evolution changes in a compact summary.
- Encounter pages gain nearby story-progress rail, route summary, linked trainers, and previous/next progression navigation.
- Trainer splits keep every battle inline; boss battles get elevated styling, trainer art, highest-level highlighting, and an area jump control.
- Move detail pages add quick effect tags and known Kaizo change callouts while retaining direct learner lists.
- Team builder adds average Speed, offensive lean, type diversity and shared-weakness warnings.
- Mobile/landscape density rules and existing Pokémon/type/category sprite systems are preserved.

Trainer-class sprites load from Pokémon Showdown's public trainer sprite directory where a suitable sprite exists, with existing fallbacks for unsupported custom trainer names/classes.
