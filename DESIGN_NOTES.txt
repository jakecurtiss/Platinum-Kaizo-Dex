Platinum Kaizo Companion v3.1

V3.1 brief
- Keeps the v1 / Origin HeartGold-derived information architecture and Pokémon-specific UI components.
- Cosmetic Sinnoh / Diamond / Pearl / Platinum redesign only outside Trainers.
- Restores and preserves circular type icons and Gen IV Physical / Special / Status thumbnails.
- Trainer section is the sole structural redesign: workbook split navigation + compact six-Pokémon battle sheets.
- Encounter tables restored from the Platinum Kaizo workbook and ordered for story progression. Broken #REF formula rows are excluded rather than displayed.
- Duplicate encounter maps remain separate and are labelled Area 1, Area 2, etc.
- Spreadsheet values are authoritative; e.g. 89 (+10) displays 89 as the Kaizo value and +10 as the vanilla delta.

Research cross-checks
- Official PKCalc describes its Dex as including Pokémon, moves, locations and automatic encounter tracking.
- PKCalc / official Lua tooling was used to cross-check canonical Sinnoh location naming.
- ROM Hack Guides' Platinum Kaizo walkthrough was used as a secondary story-order reference; its checkpoint grouping is explicitly described as a reading of story order using the game's own tables.
